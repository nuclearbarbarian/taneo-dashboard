How to Read This

Cell legend

  •   Approved. Permit issued or operational.
  •   Under Review. Application filed; agency is reviewing.
  •   Not Filed. Required but not yet submitted.
  •   Projected. Pre-application stage; permit anticipated but timing unknown.
  •   Gap. Status not publicly available.
  •   N/A. Not applicable to this project.

Why the matrix matters

The FAI playbook observes that "20 percent of permits cause 80 percent of total delay." For Texas nuclear, the binding constraints are predictable: NRC licensing dominates the federal critical path; on the state side, water rights, TPDES wastewater, and ERCOT interconnection are the most likely to push a project's commercial-operations date.

This matrix surfaces, at a glance, which agencies hold the most outstanding permits across the portfolio. If a single agency consistently shows empty cells across multiple projects, that's a process-level bottleneck — not a project-level problem.

Permit Counts by Status

The matrix counts in the "Permit Counts by Status" table exclude operational permits already in place at Texas's existing nuclear stations (Comanche Peak and South Texas Project) so that the "Approved" total reflects pipeline progress, not the legacy fleet. Existing facilities are not measured against the same critical-path standard as new builds.
